Frequently Asked Questions

What is a hacker house?

A hacker house is a shared living space where builders, developers, founders, designers, and creators live together for an intense period to build projects, collaborate deeply, and accelerate their startups. Think of it as an ashram for startup energy.

Who is HackHouse for?

HackHouse is for early-stage founders, developers, designers, and tech professionals who are actively building something, whether it's a startup, side project, or open-source tool.

How long can I stay?

The minimum stay is 1 month, and most residents stay for 2-6 months.

What's included in the ₹35,000/month?

Fully-furnished double-sharing room, 3 meals daily, 1 Gbps internet with 24x7 backup, shared access to premium AI tools, weekly events, mentorship sessions, and a curated community of 8 builders.

How do I get selected?

Apply through our form, and we'll schedule a 15-minute chat. We notify within 48 hours.

Can I try before committing?

Yes! We offer daily passes (₹500) and monthly co-working passes (₹5,000).

How is HackHouse different from a regular PG?

A PG just gives you a bed. HackHouse is a startup residency that gives you a curated community of builders, premium AI tools worth ₹20,000+/month, weekly mentorship, and demo day prep.
